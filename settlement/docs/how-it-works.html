How Our Consignment Program Works

Continental Bead Suppliers · 3035 E. Post Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89120
A plain-English walkthrough — read this before consigning. · Version dated September 2026

Thank you for considering Continental Bead Suppliers to sell your bead and jewelry-making inventory. We want to make sure you understand exactly how the program works before you send us anything. This document is a friendly summary — the actual contract has the legal language, but everything below reflects how we run the program day to day.

The Short Version

  • You ship us your inventory at your expense.
  • We catalog, photograph, list, store, sell, and ship it — in our Las Vegas showroom, on continentalbeadsuppliers.com, and on whichever marketplaces and channels we judge will sell your items best. Where and how to sell is entirely at our discretion.
  • We split the money from each sale: you get 40%, we get 60% — calculated on Net Proceeds, i.e. after the selling costs for that sale (payment processing, marketplace commissions, packaging and transaction costs) come out per our standard fee schedule.
  • We pay you via Zelle on the 1st business day of each month, with a statement showing every item that sold and the net amount credited to you. You also get a login to our online consignor portal where you can see your sales, payments, and balance any time.
  • We need your W-9 on file before we can send your first payment.
  • Important: sending us merchandise means you're agreeing to the consignment contract, whether or not you've signed it. Please read it first — it's available in the consignor portal and we'll email you a copy any time.

What Does the Math Actually Look Like?

This is the part most people want to see. Here's an example for a strand of vintage Czech glass that sells on our online store for $40. The customer pays shipping separately at checkout, so shipping doesn't touch the split at all.

Sale price (item only — customer pays shipping separately)$40.00$40.00

Your share (40% of Net Proceeds)$15.20

Your share is 40% of Net Proceeds (what's left after selling costs), not 40% of the sticker price. This is how almost every legitimate consignment program works — the alternative would be us absorbing all the fees, which would mean we'd have to take a much larger commission to make the program viable.

Selling costs vary by channel: marketplace sales (eBay, Etsy, Faire, and similar) carry higher commissions than sales through our own store or showroom, so Net Proceeds on those sales will be somewhat lower. We apply a standard fee schedule per channel that reasonably approximates what each sale costs us in third-party fees and transaction materials — the current schedule is available any time on request. Your monthly statement shows the net amount credited for every item sold.

Step-by-Step: From Your Boxes to Your Bank

Step 1 — Initial Conversation

You reach out (email, phone, or our contact form) and tell us roughly what you have. Send photos if you can. We'll give you honest feedback on whether your collection is a good fit for our program. No pressure, no obligation.

Step 2 — Inventory List & Proposed Prices

Once we agree to move forward, you'll prepare an inventory list (Exhibit A in the contract) describing your items and proposing prices for each one. Your proposed prices are your starting recommendation — we'll review them and either accept them, suggest different prices, or research comparable sold items if you're not sure what something is worth. Don't stress about getting prices perfect. We've been in this industry over 20 years and we have a strong sense of what moves at what price.

Step 3 — You Ship Us the Inventory

You cover the cost to ship inventory to us. We strongly recommend insuring the shipment — once it's with the carrier, any loss claim is between you and the carrier. Ship to:

Please do not ship freight-collect or COD — we will refuse the delivery.

Shipping your merchandise to us counts as accepting the consignment contract, even if you haven't signed it yet. We'd still love a signature for both our records — but please read the agreement before your boxes go out the door.

Step 4 — We Receive, Count, and Catalog

When your shipment arrives, we open it, count every item, and catalog it into our inventory system. We'll send you confirmation of what we received. Our count is final — if your list says 500 strands and we count 480, our count controls. We do this carefully and we're not in the habit of losing things, but we can't accept liability for items allegedly shipped but not received. Each consignor's inventory is tracked separately in our system. Your items stay attributed to you. We do not commingle your inventory with other consignors for accounting purposes.

Step 5 — We List, Photograph, Market, and Sell

This is where the patience comes in. Cataloging and listing inventory is labor-intensive. Photography, writing product descriptions, setting up listings — it takes time. Most collections take several months to fully list, and large collections (a full bead shop closure, for example) can take up to a year or longer.

We sell wherever we believe your items will move fastest: our Las Vegas showroom, our own online store, online marketplaces, social-media shops, wholesale channels to bead stores and designers, and new channels as we add them. Where, how, and at what price each item is offered is entirely our call — that flexibility is a big part of why the program works.

Step 6 — Markdowns Over Time

Beads and jewelry supplies don't always sell on the first try. To keep your inventory moving, we apply an automatic markdown schedule:

  • If an item hasn't sold within 6 months of being listed, we reduce the price by 10%.
  • If it still hasn't sold at 12 months, we reduce the price by 25% (cumulative).
  • After 12 months, we may apply additional markdowns at our discretion, including clearance pricing and bundling.

This is standard practice in retail and is how we keep money flowing to you instead of letting items sit forever at a price the market won't bear.

Step 7 — Monthly Statements & Payments

On the 1st business day of every month, you'll receive:

  • A statement listing every one of your items sold the prior month and the net amount credited to you for each, plus any refunds, adjustments, and your balance.
  • A Zelle payment for your share.

You can also log into the consignor portal any time to see your sales, payments, and current balance. Returns and chargebacks (rare, but they happen) are reconciled in the month they come back. If that ever makes your balance negative, you never owe us anything — the negative simply carries forward and is offset by your future sales.

Important Things to Know Before You Consign

Exclusivity

While your inventory is with us, you cannot sell the same merchandise anywhere else. Not on your own eBay account, not at craft fairs, not on Etsy, not through private sales. This is a real restriction and we take it seriously — it prevents us from listing an item for sale only to find out you sold it yesterday on Facebook Marketplace. If you have inventory you want to keep selling on your own, set it aside before you ship to us.

Storage & Display Space

We don't currently charge storage, holding, or display fees, and we don't plan to. But to be straight with you: if our cost of housing consigned goods grows significantly, the contract lets us introduce a display/storage fee for showroom or warehouse space. If that ever happens, you'd get at least 30 days' written notice of the amount before it starts, it would show as its own line on your monthly statement, and you'd always have the option to take your inventory back instead. If we ever relocate, we bring all consigned inventory with us at our expense.

Rejected Items

Occasionally we receive items we can't sell — damaged stock, counterfeit or trademark-infringing items, hazardous materials, or things that simply aren't a fit for our customer base. If we reject something, you have 15 days to either (a) pay for return shipping back to you, or (b) authorize us to donate the items to a charity. If we don't hear from you within 15 days, we'll donate or dispose of the rejected items.

Ending the Program

Either of us can end the agreement with 30 days' notice. If you decide to pull your remaining inventory back, you cover return shipping and you have 90 days to arrange pickup. After 90 days, anything still here is considered abandoned and becomes our property.

In the Event of Death or Incapacity

This is a sensitive subject, but we have to address it because consignment relationships often last years. If you pass away or become legally incapacitated, your executor or representative has 6 months to contact us, provide proof of authority, and tell us who should continue receiving payments. If no one contacts us within 6 months, the remaining inventory becomes our property and we have no further payment obligations. We highly recommend telling your executor, attorney, or a family member about this agreement and giving them a copy. There's a designated contact line on the contract for exactly this purpose.

Taxes & W-9

We're required by the IRS to collect a W-9 from you before sending any payments. If we pay you more than $600 in a calendar year (which is likely if your collection has any size to it), we'll issue you a 1099-NEC at year-end. You're responsible for reporting and paying any income taxes on what you receive — we don't withhold taxes from your payments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I see my first payment? It depends on how quickly your inventory sells. The first 1–3 months are typically spent cataloging and listing your items, so your first statement may show only partial inventory listed. As more of your inventory goes live, sales should pick up. Some items sell within days; some take a year. We're committed to working through your full collection.

Can I see what's selling and what's not? Yes — log into the consignor portal any time to see everything that's sold, your payments, and your balance. If you want a status update on your full unsold inventory, email us and we'll pull a report.

Can I see how the fees were calculated? Your statement shows the net credited for each item. If you'd like the fee breakdown behind any line, or a copy of our current standard fee schedule, just ask — we're happy to walk through the math.

What if I want a specific item back? Tell us in writing. If it hasn't sold yet, we can pull it from listings and return it to you at your shipping expense. If it's already sold, obviously we can't get it back.

Can I add more inventory later? Absolutely. We can amend Exhibit A by email — you don't need to sign a brand-new contract every time you find another box in storage.

Do you appraise my collection? We give honest feedback on what we think items will realistically sell for, based on our 20+ years in the industry and comparable sold listings. This is not a formal appraisal — for insurance, estate, or tax appraisal purposes you'd need a certified appraiser.

What if you go out of business? Title to unsold inventory remains with you the entire time it's with us. If something catastrophic happened to our business, the unsold inventory would remain your property, not a creditor's asset. (This is one of the legal benefits of how consignment works versus an outright sale.)
