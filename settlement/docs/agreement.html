1. Consigned Merchandise; Acceptance by Delivery

Consignor delivers to Consignee the beads, jewelry-making supplies, findings, components, finished jewelry, and related inventory described in the attached Exhibit A (the “Merchandise”). Additional inventory may be added from time to time by written or emailed amendment acknowledged by both Parties, and each such addition shall be governed by the terms of this Agreement.

Acceptance by Delivery. Shipping or delivering Merchandise to Consignee constitutes Consignor's full acceptance of, and agreement to be bound by, this Agreement and all of its terms, whether or not Consignor has signed this Agreement. The version of this Agreement most recently provided to Consignor or published by Consignee (including in Consignee's online consignor portal) as of the date of each delivery governs that delivery. Consignor's continued consignment of Merchandise after notice of any amendment constitutes acceptance of the amendment.

Consignor represents and warrants that Consignor is the lawful owner of the Merchandise, that the Merchandise is free of all liens, security interests, and encumbrances, and that Consignor has full right and authority to consign the Merchandise to Consignee for resale.

2. Inbound Shipping, Receipt, and Inventory Verification

Consignor is solely responsible for all costs of shipping, packing, freight, insurance, and handling required to deliver the Merchandise to Consignee's premises. Consignee will not accept freight-collect, COD, or similar shipments and may refuse any such delivery at Consignor's expense. Consignor shall provide a written or itemized inventory list (Exhibit A) describing the Merchandise being shipped, along with Consignor's proposed sale price for each item or lot. Consignor's proposed prices are recommendations only and are subject to acceptance, modification, or markdown by Consignee as set forth in Section 5.

Upon receipt, Consignee will inspect, count, and catalog the Merchandise within a commercially reasonable period. Consignee's inventory count, descriptions, and condition assessments are final and binding on the Parties. Any discrepancy between Consignor's list and Consignee's count shall be resolved in favor of Consignee's count. Consignor waives any claim against Consignee for items allegedly shipped but not received, lost in transit, or damaged in transit, except to the extent recoverable from the freight carrier directly (which Consignor may pursue at Consignor's own expense).

3. Non-Commingling and Segregated Tracking

Consignee will track each Consignor's Merchandise separately and will not commingle Consignor's Merchandise with inventory belonging to Consignee or to other consignors for purposes of revenue accounting. Each item or lot will be assigned identifiers in Consignee's inventory system tying that item back to Consignor so that sales can be attributed and accounted for under Section 6.

4. Title and Risk of Loss

Title to the Merchandise remains with Consignor until the Merchandise is sold to an end customer, at which point title passes directly from Consignor to the end customer. Consignee acts solely as a sales agent for the Merchandise and acquires no ownership interest in any unsold Merchandise, except as expressly provided in Sections 8 (Rejected Merchandise), 10 (Term and Termination), and 16 (Death or Incapacity of Consignor). Consignor bears the risk of loss for damage, theft, fire, flood, or other casualty while the Merchandise is in Consignee's possession, except to the extent any such loss is caused by Consignee's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Consignee is not required to maintain insurance on the Merchandise, and Consignor is encouraged to maintain its own insurance coverage if desired.

5. Pricing, Markdowns, Listing, and Sales Channels

Consignor may propose an initial sale price for each item or lot of Merchandise on Exhibit A. Consignee may accept Consignor's proposed price, propose an alternative price (which Consignor may accept or withdraw the item), or, where Consignor has not provided a proposed price, set the initial price in Consignee's discretion based on Consignee's research (including but not limited to comparable Google search results and completed eBay listings). Once an item is listed for sale, Consignor's prior approval is not required for any subsequent price change made under the markdown schedule below or otherwise pursuant to this Section.

Automatic Markdown Schedule. If an item has not sold within the following time periods measured from the date it is first listed for sale by Consignee, Consignee may, without further notice or consent, reduce the listed price as follows: (a) 10% markdown after six (6) months unsold; (b) 25% cumulative markdown after twelve (12) months unsold; and (c) additional markdowns at Consignee's sole discretion thereafter, including clearance pricing, bundling, and promotional pricing, as Consignee determines necessary to sell the item.

Sales Channels. Consignee has full and sole discretion over where, how, and through which channels the Merchandise is offered and sold, and over how it is photographed, described, listed, marketed, advertised, and packaged. Channels may include, without limitation, Consignee's retail showroom, Consignee's e-commerce store, online marketplaces, wholesale channels, social-media platforms, in-person events, and any other channel now existing or later adopted by Consignee. No provision of this Agreement obligates Consignee to use, or to refrain from using, any particular channel.

Consignor acknowledges that listing, photographing, cataloging, and selling consigned inventory is labor-intensive and that it may take Consignee several months, and in some cases up to one year or longer, to list all Merchandise for sale. Consignor agrees that no timeline or expectation is created regarding when any particular item will be listed or sold.

6. Revenue Split and Compensation

For each unit of Merchandise sold, the Parties shall calculate “Net Proceeds” as follows: Net Proceeds = Gross Sale Price minus Selling Costs.

“Gross Sale Price” means the price paid by the end customer for the item itself, excluding any amounts the customer pays for shipping and excluding sales taxes collected. Shipping amounts paid by customers are collected and retained by Consignee, are not part of Gross Sale Price, and shipping and fulfillment costs are not deducted as Selling Costs. Shipping does not affect the revenue split in either direction.

“Selling Costs” means the per-sale selling costs determined under Consignee's standardized fee schedule then in effect for the channel on which the item sold. The standardized fee schedule is set by Consignee to reasonably approximate the third-party and transaction costs attributable to sales on each channel — including payment processing fees, marketplace and platform commissions, mandatory per-sale advertising fees, packaging and transaction materials, and chargebacks, reversals, refunds, and returns attributable to a sale. Consignee may update the standardized fee schedule from time to time to reflect changes in underlying costs; the schedule in effect on the date of each sale applies to that sale. The current standardized fee schedule is available to Consignor upon written request. Selling Costs do not include Consignee's general operating expenses, rent, labor, photography, marketing, or overhead.

After Selling Costs are subtracted, Net Proceeds shall be divided as follows: (a) sixty percent (60%) to Consignee as its consignment commission; and (b) forty percent (40%) to Consignor as Consignor's share of Net Proceeds. Consignor acknowledges and agrees that the 40% share is calculated on Net Proceeds (after Selling Costs), not on Gross Sale Price.

Additional detail regarding the calculation of any line, including the applicable standardized fee schedule, is available to Consignor upon written request. Consignee may also provide Consignor with access to an online consignor portal showing Consignor's sales, payments, and balance.

Consignee shall remit Consignor's share on the first (1st) business day of each month for sales completed during the prior calendar month. 8. Inspection, Acceptance, and Rejection of Merchandise

Consignee shall have a reasonable period after receipt to inspect the Merchandise. Consignee reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to reject any items it determines to be damaged, contaminated, hazardous, mislabeled, counterfeit, infringing on intellectual property rights, unsalable, not commercially viable for Consignee's sales channels, or otherwise unsuitable for resale. With respect to rejected Merchandise, Consignor may elect, within fifteen (15) days of Consignee's written notice of rejection, to either: (a) arrange and pay for the return shipment of rejected items at Consignor's sole expense (including providing a prepaid shipping label or remitting funds sufficient to cover shipping); or (b) authorize Consignee to donate the rejected items to a charity or organization of Consignee's choosing. If Consignor fails to respond within fifteen (15) days, or fails to arrange return shipping within thirty (30) days, the rejected items shall be deemed abandoned and may be donated or otherwise disposed of by Consignee without further obligation or accounting to Consignor.

9. Exclusivity

During the term of this Agreement and while any Merchandise remains in Consignee's possession, Consignor shall not, directly or indirectly, list, market, advertise, sell, or offer to sell the consigned Merchandise (or any portion of it) through any other channel, marketplace, retailer, wholesaler, auction house, online platform, social media listing, classified listing, or private sale. Consignor agrees that the Merchandise consigned under this Agreement is offered to Consignee on an exclusive basis. If Consignor breaches this exclusivity, Consignee may, in addition to any other remedy, immediately terminate this Agreement, retain any Merchandise then in its possession until accounting is settled, and offset any losses, lost commissions, or duplicative listing costs against amounts otherwise payable to Consignor.

10. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date (or the date of Consignor's first delivery of Merchandise, if earlier) and continues until terminated by either Party. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party. Upon termination, Consignor may request return of any unsold Merchandise. Consignor is solely responsible for all costs of return shipping, packing, and handling, and must arrange pickup or provide a prepaid shipping label within thirty (30) days of termination. Any Merchandise not retrieved within ninety (90) days following termination shall be deemed abandoned, and Consignee may, at its sole discretion, retain, sell, liquidate, or otherwise dispose of such Merchandise without further obligation or accounting to Consignor. Amounts already paid through the most recent monthly settlement under Section 7 are not refundable.

11. Storage, Location, and Space Fees

Consignee will store the Merchandise at its premises located at 3035 E. Post Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89120, or at such other premises as Consignee may relocate to from time to time. Consignee will transport the Merchandise to any new premises along with its own inventory in the event of a relocation, at Consignee's expense.

Consignee does not currently charge storage, holding, or display fees. However, Consignee reserves the right, at any time and in its sole discretion, to begin charging Consignor a recurring fee for showroom display space, storage, or warehousing of the Merchandise if Consignee deems it necessary (including due to increases in Consignee's cost of housing the Merchandise). Any such fee will take effect no sooner than thirty (30) days after written or emailed notice to Consignor stating the amount, will be itemized on Consignor's monthly statement, and will be deducted from amounts otherwise payable to Consignor (or carried against Consignor's account balance). Upon receiving notice of such a fee, Consignor may terminate this Agreement under Section 10 and retrieve the Merchandise before the fee takes effect.

15. Limitation of Liability

In no event shall Consignee be liable to Consignor for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, including lost profits, arising out of or related to this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages. Consignee's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the amounts paid or payable to Consignor during the six (6) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

16. Death or Incapacity of Consignor

In the event of Consignor's death or legal incapacity, Consignor's executor, administrator, personal representative, legal guardian, or other duly authorized representative (the “Representative”) must contact Consignee in writing within six (6) months of the date of death or incapacity to (i) provide reasonable documentation of authority to act on behalf of Consignor or Consignor's estate (such as letters testamentary, letters of administration, or equivalent), and (ii) designate the person or entity to whom continuing payments under this Agreement shall be made (the “Successor Payee”).

If a Representative does not contact Consignee within six (6) months of Consignor's death or incapacity, or if no Representative can be identified, then all unsold Merchandise then held by Consignee shall, automatically and without further action, become the sole property of Consignee, free and clear of any claim by Consignor's estate, heirs, beneficiaries, or assigns. Consignee shall have no obligation to make any further payment to Consignor, Consignor's estate, or any other party with respect to such Merchandise, and Consignee shall have no obligation to locate, notify, or investigate the existence of any Representative. Consignor acknowledges that this provision is a material term of this Agreement and is a condition of Consignee's willingness to accept and warehouse the Merchandise on a long-term basis. Consignor is encouraged to provide a copy of this Agreement to Consignor's executor, attorney, or family members so that they are aware of the six-month notification window.
